A good wedding suit does not have to become a souvenir that spends the next decade hanging untouched in a wardrobe.

Choosing cloth, colour and styling with life after the wedding in mind can make the finished suit far more useful.

## Think beyond one day

Depending on the design, the jacket, trousers and waistcoat can often be worn separately as well as together. That gives the suit a life well beyond the ceremony itself.

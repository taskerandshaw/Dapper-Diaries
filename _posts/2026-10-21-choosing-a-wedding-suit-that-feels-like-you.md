A wedding suit should feel special without making you feel like somebody else.

The best starting point is not a trend, a Pinterest board or somebody else's wedding photographs. It is the setting, the season, how formal the day will feel and what you already know you enjoy wearing.

## Start with the person, not the suit

Colour, cloth and cut all matter, but confidence matters more. A well-chosen wedding suit should work with the rest of the day while still feeling unmistakably yours.

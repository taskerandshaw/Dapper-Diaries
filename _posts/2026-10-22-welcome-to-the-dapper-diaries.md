The Dapper Diaries is where we talk about clothing without making it more complicated than it needs to be.

At Tasker & Shaw, our days are spent talking to people about fit, cloth, proportion, weddings, work, confidence and the simple question of what makes someone feel good when they get dressed. This is where we can take those conversations a little further.

## Proper clothes, useful advice

There will be practical guides to made-to-measure tailoring, thoughts on personal style, wedding advice, cloth stories, legal dress and women’s tailoring, alongside the occasional look behind the scenes at Tasker & Shaw.

The aim is not to tell you what you are *supposed* to wear. It is to help you understand your options, make better choices and enjoy your clothes more.

If there is something you would like us to cover, we would love to hear it.

There is something about a really good shirt that you notice the moment you put it on.

It is not necessarily dramatic. In fact, that is part of the appeal.

The collar sits properly. The cuffs finish where they should. The shirt follows the body without clinging to it, and there is enough room to move without carrying unnecessary fabric around the waist, back or sleeves.

It simply feels right.

That is the power of a custom-made shirt.

## Fit Changes Everything

Ready-to-wear shirts have to work around standard sizing. That means a particular collar size usually comes with an assumed chest, waist, shoulder width and sleeve length.

For some people, that works perfectly well.

For many others, it means compromise.

The collar fits but the body is too loose. The chest feels comfortable but the sleeves are too long. The shoulders pull, the cuffs disappear beneath the jacket sleeve, or the shirt balloons around the waist.

A custom-made shirt starts with the person instead.

Measurements matter, of course, but so does the way somebody actually wears a shirt. Some people prefer a little more room. Others want a cleaner, closer shape. Some need additional space through the shoulders or chest, while others want the waist brought in without feeling restricted.

The goal is not to make a shirt tight.

It is to make it balanced.

## Luxury You Can Feel

A shirt spends more time directly against your skin than almost anything else you wear, so the cloth matters.

Good shirting fabric feels different.

Fine cottons are smooth, breathable and comfortable, and the quality becomes particularly noticeable over a long day. The shirt should move with you, sit comfortably beneath a jacket and feel as good at the end of the day as it did when you first put it on.

This is one of those areas where luxury is not really about showing anybody else what you have bought.

It is about how it feels to wear.

There is a quiet pleasure in putting on something made from beautiful cloth that fits properly and has been made specifically for you.

You may be the only person who fully appreciates the difference.

But you will appreciate it every time you wear it.

## The Details Make It Yours

Made-to-measure also gives you the opportunity to make the shirt feel personal.

Collar style, cuff choice, buttons, contrast stitching, monograms and cloth all change the character of the finished shirt.

You might want a crisp white shirt designed specifically to sit beneath a business suit. You may want something softer and more relaxed for weekends, or a shirt chosen to work perfectly with a wedding suit.

None of those choices need to be loud.

Often the best details are the ones that simply make the shirt feel more like yours.

## More Than an Afterthought

It is easy to spend hours choosing a suit and then treat the shirt underneath it as something secondary.

We think that is a mistake.

The shirt frames the face, affects the way the jacket sits around the neck and collar, and plays a huge part in how comfortable the whole outfit feels.

A beautifully made suit deserves a shirt that works with it rather than against it.

And once you have experienced a shirt that fits properly, feels luxurious against the skin and has been made around you, it becomes very difficult to look at standard sizing in quite the same way again.
